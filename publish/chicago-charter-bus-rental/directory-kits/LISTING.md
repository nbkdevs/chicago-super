### Description (132 words)

Chicago Super Coach is a charter bus operator in Elk Grove Village, Illinois, serving Chicago, its suburbs and out-of-state trips. Unlike a booking marketplace, it quotes and sends the vehicles it owns, so the bus you are shown is the bus that arrives.

The fleet has four sizes: an Executive Sprinter Van (13-15 passengers), a Small Coach Bus (22-28), a Coach Bus (39-44) and a SuperCoach (50-57). Drivers are licensed, experienced and drug tested, transportation is insured, and every coach seat has a seatbelt.

People use it for corporate shuttles, school field trips, wedding guest transport, airport runs and sporting events. If you are comparing options for a Chicago Charter Bus Rental, the site has fleet details, a two-step quote form and a phone line (630-624-3448) for last-minute questions. Charters operate 24/7.
